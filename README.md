## 🕹️ About

Nighthawks is a story-rich vampire RPG blending adventure, visual-novel, and interactive-fiction elements. Six months after dying and being reborn as a vampire, you arrive in a city where the supernatural has been exposed and take control of a failing nightclub. Build a nightlife empire through seduction, intimidation, recruitment, and supernatural Gifts while navigating suspicion, alliances, romance, betrayal, and dark humor. Developed by The Curiosity Engine and published by Wadjet Eye Games, Nighthawks features a fully voiced world with more than 800,000 words of branching stories and secrets.

Nighthawks is for players who enjoy atmospheric vampire fiction, branching RPG narratives, character-driven choices, and darkly humorous interactive storytelling.

## ✨ Features

- 🧛 **Modern Vampire RPG** — Shape your second life as a newly reborn vampire in a changing supernatural society.
- 🗣️ **Choice-Driven Stories** — Make consequential decisions across a richly written world of dark comedy and drama.
- 🩸 **Moral Consequences** — Choose between building community, satisfying your hunger, and pursuing personal ambition.
- 🎙️ **Fully Voiced World** — Explore a neon-lit setting brought to life through voiced characters and atmospheric presentation.
- 🌃 **Nightclub Empire** — Transform a failing club into a thriving refuge, business, or instrument of power.
- 🤝 **Recruitable Companions** — Meet and recruit ten distinctive outcasts, each with personal stories and useful talents.
- 📚 **Vast Narrative** — Uncover more than 800,000 words of stories, secrets, mysteries, and character moments.
- ✨ **Supernatural Gifts** — Use vampire powers such as Mesmerism, Natural Dominion, and Tenebrous Form.
